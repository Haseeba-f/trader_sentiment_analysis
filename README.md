## Project Objective

This project analyzes how Bitcoin market sentiment influences trader behavior and profitability on Hyperliquid. By combining the Fear/Greed Index with historical trade execution data, the analysis identifies behavioral patterns, evaluates sentiment-driven performance differences, and proposes actionable trading strategies.

The workflow covers:
- Data preparation and feature engineering
- Exploratory and statistical analysis
- Trader segmentation
- Predictive modeling
- Strategy recommendations based on observed patterns

## Analysis Workflow

### Part A — Data Preparation

Both datasets were cleaned and aligned at the daily level:
- Timestamp parsing and normalization (unix-ms to date)
- Removal of null PnL rows and duplicates
- Fee normalization and regime grouping (Fear / Neutral / Greed)
- Inner join merge on date

Engineered daily trader-level metrics: net PnL, win rate, trade frequency, average position size, long/short ratio, and a drawdown proxy.

### Part B — Analysis

**Performance across regimes** — median PnL, win rate, and drawdown compared across Fear, Neutral, and Greed using summary statistics and Welch's t-tests.

**Behavioral analysis** — how sentiment affects trading frequency, directional bias (long vs short), and position sizing.

**Trader segmentation** — three approaches:
- High-size vs low-size traders (median split)
- High-frequency vs low-frequency traders (median split)
- Consistent Winners vs Consistent Losers (top/bottom quartile win rate + cumulative PnL)

### Part C — Strategy Recommendations

Three sentiment-aware trading rules with triggers, rules, and evidence. See `writeup.md` for full detail.

---

## Predictive Modeling (Bonus)

A Gradient Boosting classifier was trained using a Scikit-learn pipeline (imputation → scaling → SelectKBest → GBM) to predict next-day trader profitability. Features include trade count, win rate, average position size, sentiment regime, directional bias, and PnL volatility.

---

## Behavioral Clustering (Bonus)

K-Means clustering (k=4, selected via elbow method) identifies four distinct trader behavioral archetypes. PCA visualization is used to interpret cluster separation.

---

## Key Findings

- Fear regimes show approximately 2.2× higher median daily PnL than Greed regimes
- High-size traders experience significantly larger performance swings across sentiment regimes
- Trader activity levels are strongly associated with next-day profitability outcomes
- Consistent Winners maintain positive performance across all regimes; weaker traders are more exposed to sentiment-driven volatility

---

## Strategy Recommendations

| Strategy | Trigger | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Sentiment-based position size cap | Fear / Extreme Fear | Limit size to ≤50% of trailing 30-day average for lower-performing traders |
| Activity moderation | Fear regimes | Reduce excessive trade frequency for weaker traders |
| Contrarian long | Extreme Fear | Consistent Winners may selectively deploy longs with strict stop-loss controls |

---

## Limitations

This analysis is based on historical trade execution data and sentiment classification only. External factors such as macroeconomic news, liquidity conditions, and cross-asset volatility were not incorporated. Future work could integrate additional market indicators for a more comprehensive model.
